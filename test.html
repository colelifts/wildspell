A colorful fantasy card duel with spells, status effects, stacking, minigames, and animated heroes.

WildSpell Rules

Match the discard by color, number, or spell. Draw when nothing matches.

Freeze skips the opponent. Rewind gives you another turn. Arcane +2 and Chaos +4 stack.

Arsonist applies Burn. Playing red removes Burn; failing to answer makes it spread and draw cards.

Whirlwind Swap trades random cards. Stormcall demands yellow. Frostbite locks a random card.

Call Final Card before playing down to one card. In Wild Mode, both players enter a short challenge. Lose it and draw two.
