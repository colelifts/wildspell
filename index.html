How to Play

Match the top discard by color, number, or action. Skip removes the opponent’s turn. Reverse acts like Skip in a two-player duel. Draw Two and Wild Draw Four force extra cards and skip the opponent. Wild lets you choose the next color. After drawing, you may immediately play the drawn card when legal or pass. Press FINAL CARD before playing down to one card. Forgetting the call gives you a two-card penalty. Win rounds to earn the value of cards left in your opponent’s hand. First to 200 wins the match.
